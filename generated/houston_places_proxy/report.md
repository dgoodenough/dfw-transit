## Method notes and limits

- Value is a sketch: commutes between station catchments of one line (walk, and park & ride at regional-rail stations and termini), each pair's transit share from the config.json logit against driving. Trips that need a transfer are not credited to a line, so the network effect (and the full model's feeder buses) appear only in the model run.
- Corridors connect detected centers: a dense residential arterial that links no two centers is not a candidate (the hand plans' residential BRT grids are mostly missed).
- Geometry is the routed corridor, not a design alignment (`python -m dfwt align` fits one).
- New lines open together (a potential network, not a staged plan).
