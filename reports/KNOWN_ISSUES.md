# Known issues and backlog (v2)

Live validation output is in `VALIDATION.md` (regenerated by every build).

## Model limits
- **Demand is synthetic.** The LODES origin-destination file isn't in the repo
  (and couldn't be downloaded from the build environment), so commute flows come
  from a gravity model fitted to a ~17 km mean commute. Rankings and plan-vs-plan
  comparisons are more trustworthy than absolute counts. Fix: drop LODES OD in
  `data/raw/od_tracts.csv` (see `data/README.md`) and rebuild.
- **Commutes only.** LODES covers home-to-work trips, so Stockyards, stadium,
  airport and event demand is undercounted. This matters most for the Fort Worth
  core (one reason step A3 kept the Stockyards/Panther Island leg in 2035).
- **No bus network, no equity data.** There's no car ownership, income or
  safety data yet (panel step A12).
- **No growth.** Population and jobs are fixed at 2020/2022 levels. NCTCOG
  2045/2050 forecasts would re-weight outer lines.

## Geometry
- **Sketch alignments.** These v2 additions are drawn station to station, not
  routed on real right-of-way: SH-121 BRT (E38), McKinney Line → Parker Road,
  Midlothian → Westmoreland, Jacksboro BRT via T&P. Each needs a ROW routing pass.
- **Hairpins inherited from v1**: Teal at Woodhaven and Sundance Square, Red at
  Waterside, Berry Line at Hemphill/Berry. These are probably intended branch
  reversals; confirm or re-draw.
- **Geometry gaps**: SE TexRail (2.2 km), Gainesville (1.6 km) and FTW/Denton
  (0.5 km) end short of Fort Worth Central (the v1 "hub snap" moved the stop but
  not the line).
- **Station names**: 31 POI-style names were replaced with approximate
  neighborhood or cross-street names (see `network/CHANGELOG.md`). Many
  directional-suffix names ("Fair Park E7", "Uptown") remain.
