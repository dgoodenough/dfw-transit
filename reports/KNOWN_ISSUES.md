# Known issues and backlog

Live validation output is in `VALIDATION.md` (regenerated by every build). What changed and
when is in `network/CHANGELOG.md`. Last reviewed after v2.15.

## Data
- **Jobs and commute flows are estimated.** LODES isn't in the repo and its host
  (lehd.ces.census.gov) is blocked from the build environment, so commute flows come from a
  gravity model fitted to a ~17 km mean commute, calibrated to today's ~80k rail boardings.
  Comparisons between plans and lines are more trustworthy than absolute counts. Fix: put
  LODES OD in `data/raw/od_tracts.csv` (see `data/README.md`) and rebuild.
- **Commutes only.** The model scales commutes to all trips the way today's riders split.
  Evening, weekend, event, airport and visitor travel isn't modelled on its own, so the
  Stockyards, stadiums and DFW Airport are undercounted.
- **County-led growth uses assumed weights**, not NCTCOG's county forecasts (Dallas 0.5,
  Tarrant 1.0, Collin/Denton 1.8, other counties 2.0; `growth.alt.county_weights`).
- **Mobility 2050's $57.9B** for rail and bus very likely includes running today's systems;
  not confirmed against NCTCOG's full financial chapter.

## Model
- **Shared track:** a rider waits for one line, not for whichever train on a shared trunk
  comes first (about +2% ridership when fixed). Needs trunk services in the routing graph,
  splitting their riders back to lines, the same change in the map's trip planner, and a
  recalibration.
- **Route choice and the slow-trip penalty:** riders pick paths by perceived minutes, but
  the mode choice also penalises trips much slower than driving (`b_ratio`), which the path
  choice doesn't see. Some southern Dallas BRTs come out negative because of it
  (Illinois-Lake June, Kiest-Ledbetter). Changing it needs a recalibration.
- **Line values** are measured on the final year's land use with everything open; values are
  not re-measured after small geometry edits until the next `python -m dfwt values`.
- **4-car trains** on the I-20 / I-30 trunk carry their extra operating cost from opening,
  though the longer platforms come in 2060.

## Geometry and diagram
- **Walnut Hill / Stemmons** (Stemmons-Brookhollow BRT) is reached by a detour longer than
  `tools/despur.py` moves a stop (700 m); its stop should move or the route change.
- Train reversals flagged as HAIRPIN are real: TRE at Fort Worth T&P and Alvarado, the
  Mansfield-Denton-Carrollton through-route at Downtown Denton, SH-114 at Freeport.
  Buses pulling into rail stations (Mockingbird / John W Carpenter, Frisco Main St) are too.
- Corridors follow streets and track; they are not designed alignments (CURVE_RADIUS warnings).
- **Diagram:** Fort Worth Central and Fort Worth T&P each show as two nodes (regional rail
  and metro platforms; merging them exceeds what one node can draw), the Gainesville Line
  crosses the SH-114 Line west of Grapevine, and the Red Line turns sharply at Parker Road.
