# Results

Generated by `python -m dfwt build`. Do not edit by hand.

Demand source: **gravity** (gravity model from 2020 census population and 2022 LODES jobs, fitted to a ~17 km mean commute; drop LODES OD into `data/raw/od_tracts.csv` to use real flows). Trust comparisons between plans and lines more than absolute counts.

Definitions: *competitive commute* = transit door-to-door time no more than 1.3x the peak drive + 5 min (under 90 min, over 2 km). *Jobs in 45 min* = jobs reachable by transit in 45 minutes, averaged over every resident of the nine counties. *Coverage* = residents within 800 m of a metro/BRT stop or 1.6 km of a commuter rail station. Costs are 2025 USD; shared track is charged once. All assumptions live in `config.json`.
